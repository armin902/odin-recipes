Ice Cream Cake

Description

This is an amazing recipe for anyone craving a sweet treat! What makes This ice cream cake recipe is that there are multiple possible combinations possible. This recipe covers a chocolate base and chocolate ice cream cake base, but variations such as strawberry, mint chocolate chip, vanilla, and caramel are only a few possible ones. For only 20 minutes of prep time, 30 minutes of cook time, it totals out to 50 minutes for 18 servings.
