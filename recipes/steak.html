Sirloin Steak with Garlic Butter

Description

This is a delicious sirloin steak recipe that incorporates a garlic butter sauce, making the steak juicy and tender to eat. This is a relatively simple recipe, with 20 minutes of prep time, 10 minutes of cook time, totaling to only 30 minutes for 8 servings. Serving this dish with a dinner side such as mashed potatoes or asparagus makes for a great combo!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 4 pounds beef top sirloin steaks
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Collect all the Ingredients. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  2. Melt the butter into a small saucepan over medium-low heat.
  3. Stir in minced garlic and garlic powder. Set aside.
  4. Season both sides of each steak with salt and pepper.
  5. Place steaks onto a preheated grill and cook 4 to 5 minutes per side. Insert an instant-read thermometer into the center, and ensure to read 140 degrees F (60 degrees C) for medium doneness.
  6. Transfer steaks to warmed plates; brush the tops with garlic butter and allow to rest for 2-3 minutes before serving.
  7. Enjoy!
